Commodity and asset-class rankings by Sharpe ratio

Sharpe ratio here is annualised return divided by annualised volatility (no risk-free rate), net of costs, as in the dashboards. Colour = sector (asset class). Ranks are 1 = best. Mini-portfolios blend a commodity's five sleeves by Equal Weight (EW-5) or Inverse Vol (IV-5, 63-day, lagged one day).
