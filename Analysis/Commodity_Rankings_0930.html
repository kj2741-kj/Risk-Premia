Commodity and asset-class rankings by Sharpe ratio

Sharpe ratio here is annualised return divided by annualised volatility (no risk-free rate), net of costs, as in the dashboards. Colour = sector (bucket: Metals, Petroleum, Gas and Emissions, NGL, Precious, Agriculture). Basis: the 2012-start dashboard (see its Notes tab for the data end date). Ranks are 1 = best. Mini-portfolios blend a commodity's five sleeves by Equal Weight (EW-5) or Inverse Vol (IV-5, 63-day, lagged one day).

1. Buckets (sector portfolios)

Equal-weighted across each bucket's commodities, from the dashboard tabs. Click any column heading to sort (click again to reverse); the table starts sorted by the EW PORT. The small number is the rank among the six rows for that column.

2. Commodities

Click any column heading to sort by it (click again to reverse); the table starts sorted by the EW mini-portfolio. Cell colour: green positive, red negative; the small number is the rank among the 25 commodities for that column.
