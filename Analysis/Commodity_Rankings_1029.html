Commodity and asset-class rankings by Sharpe ratio

Sharpe ratio here is annualised return divided by annualised volatility (no risk-free rate), net of costs, as in the dashboards. Colour = sector (bucket: Metals, Petroleum, Gas and Emissions, NGL, Precious, Agriculture, US Power). Basis: the 2012-start dashboard (see its Notes tab for the data end date); US Power reports from 2017 (its data start in 2015 and Value needs two years of F3), so its Full column starts in 2017 and its P1 cells are empty. Ranks are 1 = best. Mini-portfolios blend a commodity's five sleeves by Equal Weight (EW-5) or Inverse Vol (IV-5, 63-day, lagged one day).

1. Buckets (sector portfolios)

Equal-weighted across each bucket's commodities, from the dashboard tabs. Click any column heading to sort (click again to reverse); the table starts sorted by the EW PORT. The small number is the rank among the seven rows for that column (six in P1, where US Power has no value).

2. Commodities

Click any column heading to sort by it (click again to reverse); the table starts sorted by the EW mini-portfolio. Cell colour: green positive, red negative; the small number is the rank among the 32 commodities for that column (27 in P1, where the five US Power series have no value). The last two columns are the Calendar-2025 liquidity snapshot and do not depend on the Period: Time to trade 1 sigma risk (in Days) = K / x with K = USD 1M of one-sigma daily risk, and Risk Millions per day = x, the USD millions of one-sigma risk the market trades per day (average daily volume x price x volatility x contract multiplier). For both, rank 1 = most liquid (fewest days, most risk per day), and the cells are not coloured.
