Risk Premia: Final Research Dashboard

Five asset classes, four sleeves each (Momentum, Carry, Carry-Momentum, Value), combined by Equal Weight and Inverse Vol only. Log-return methodology, 5 bps costs, reporting from 2011-01-01. Generated 2026-10-10.

Method and notes

Sleeves (per product)

  • Momentum: average of the signs of three moving-average crossovers, (1,20), (5,60), (20,250), on the raw front-month price; 1-day lag.
  • Carry (V2): z-score of the curve slope, (near - far) / near, against its own 252-day history. When an asset class has a short and a long tenor pair the two signs are blended at the signal level and the lag is applied once. This is the 10 Oct rule (V2 only, no V1).
  • Carry-Momentum (V3): sign of the 20-day change in the same slope, blended across the same tenor pairs. Unchanged by the 10 Oct rule.
  • Value: F3 price against its own 1,260-day average, long below -10%, short above +10%.

Tenor pairs

  • Metals and Energy: F1-F3 and F1-F13. NGL: F2-F4 and F2-F14. Precious: F1-F3 only. Agriculture: F1-F2 only (no long pair defined yet).

How things are combined

  • Products inside a sleeve are equally weighted, keeping only dates on which every product trades (a dropped date's return is carried into the next shared date, as in the project's cross-asset engine).
  • Each asset class portfolio is the four sleeves combined by EW PORT (equal weight) or Inverse Vol (weights from each sleeve's trailing 63-day volatility, recomputed daily).
  • The combined tabs are two-stage: sleeves into an asset-class portfolio, then the five asset-class portfolios into one book. All four method pairs are shown (EW or Inverse Vol at each stage), once with Energy including NatGas and once without. Asset-class series are aligned on common dates before weighting.

Data and calendars

  • Precious uses the aligned roll calendar (only the months Bloomberg's F1..Fn uses); the engine's PRECIOUS_CALENDAR_FILE now points to it. Agriculture uses the Bloomberg-matched calendar, which is identical to the existing one for five of its six products and adds Sugar.
  • Metals, Energy and NGL still use the original calendar. Their Bloomberg months have not been checked, because no single-contract monthly files exist for them.
  • Data end dates differ: Metals, Energy, NGL and Precious end 2026-06-30; Agriculture ends 2026-09-23. The combined books stop at the earliest end date and start when NGL has data (2009-11-10), so their 2026 year-to-date is shorter than the Agriculture tab's.

Cross-checks

  • Momentum and Value for Metals, Energy and NGL reproduce the published Strategy Results Dashboard to three decimals. Precious differs only through the new calendar.
  • V2-only Carry reproduces the figures in the 10 Oct README for Metals (0.124), Energy ex-NatGas (0.277) and NGL (0.683).

Cautions

  • NGL tenors are provisional (F2 reintroduces heating-season seasonality). Platinum and Palladium F3 liquidity is weaker than Gold and Silver.
  • The roll engine treats a contract whose last trade date falls after the last price date as expiring on the last price date, which rolls each product's final week early. It affects every asset class, so comparisons between tabs are not distorted by it.
  • Carry rule differences were not tested for significance, and the edge is regime dependent (see the README).
