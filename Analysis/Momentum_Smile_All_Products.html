Headline

Every single one of the 20 products — Metals, Precious Metals, NGL, Energy alike — shows a positive quadratic (smile) coefficient and a monotonic Range-bound < Middle < Big-move return ordering. Nothing else checked in this whole replication effort (regime analysis, book-figure magnitudes, tenor ordering) was this universal — consistent with the smile being closer to a mechanical property of trend-following than an empirical fact specific to any one asset or sample period.
