Momentum Smile — WTI SHAPE CONFIRMED

Bouchouev's Fig 5.4 (WTI, 2000–2022): momentum returns plotted against the underlying's own returns form a smile — momentum does well when oil makes a big move in either direction, and poorly when the market is flat/range-bound. Our sample is 2006–2026 (different vendor, only partial overlap with his window) — this tests whether the shape survives that, not whether the numbers match.

A positive x² coefficient means the curve opens upward — a smile, not a frown. Ours is +1.44 (R²=0.36, meaningful for monthly-frequency data with this much noise). The curve dips negative when the underlying return is near zero and rises on both the left (big down months) and right (big up months) — the same qualitative shape Bouchouev describes.

Binned view: average momentum return by underlying-return bucket

Months sorted into 8 equal-sized buckets by underlying return, average momentum return per bucket — the smile in bar form.

The two most extreme buckets (biggest down months, biggest up months) are the only two with clearly positive average momentum returns (+12.0% and +9.9%). Every bucket in between is flat-to-negative, with the worst sitting right around zero underlying return (−7.3%) — textbook smile: high at both tails, a dip in the middle.

Verdict

The smile shape replicates cleanly despite the price-data disparity — as expected going in, since this shape is close to a mechanical property of trend-following (whipsawed in chop, rewarded in sustained moves) rather than a specific empirical fact about his exact sample. This is the strongest structural match of everything checked so far. Our sample (2006–2026) only partially overlaps his (2000–2022) — we're missing his 2000–2006 window and he's missing our 2023–2026 window — but the mechanism clearly doesn't care.
Methodology: daily $ PnL (position × F1_continuous price change, net of 5bps TC) normalized by prior-day raw price to a daily % return proxy, compounded to monthly. Underlying monthly return = compounded daily % change of F1_raw. Quadratic fit: momentum_return = c0 + c1·underlying + c2·underlying² (fractional units). Generated 2026-07-22.
