1. Overview

Value is a long-run mean-reversion signal: it compares one point on the futures curve to that same point's own trailing moving average, and bets the price reverts toward that average when the deviation is large enough to clear a threshold. The baseline uses F8 as the anchor, deliberately further from the front of the curve than Momentum and Carry (which both operate on F1/F2/F3). Moving the anchor to F1, F2, or F3 asks whether the near-contract price mean-reverts to its own 5-year moving average instead — a different, shorter-horizon-feeling question than F8's curve-structural read, even though the lookback window (1260 trading days) and threshold (±10%) stay identical.

Precious Metals' own Carry tenor pair is now F1-F3 (changed 2026-08-03 from F1-F2, for consistency with Metals/Energy's F1-F3 near leg) — despite a real-volume liquidity check finding that Platinum and Palladium are only genuinely liquid through F1-F2 (their F3 fails at 87.0%/68.5% days traded, below the ≥90% bar). This asset class still has only 4 locked strategies (Momentum, Carry F1-F3, CarryMom F1-F3, Value), not 6, since there is still only one tenor pair. The portfolio combine methods therefore combine 4 sleeves here, not 6.

1.1 What's held fixed across all four variants (F8 baseline, F1, F2, F3)

  • Momentum: 3-way MA-crossover composite, (1,20)/(5,60)/(20,250), shift_n=1
  • Carry V1(Level)+V2(Z-score) composite: F1-F3 tenor pair (Precious Metals' single locked pair, changed 2026-08-03 from F1-F2), z-window=252, shift_n=1
  • Carry-Momentum (V3): F1-F3 tenor pair, horizon=20, shift_n=1
  • Value lookback (1260d), threshold (±10%), execution lag (shift_n=2) — only the anchor CONTRACT changes
  • Transaction cost: 5bps round-trip. Products: Gold, Silver, Copper_COMEX, Platinum, Palladium, equal-weighted. Regimes: R1 Supercycle Unwind (2011-2015), R2 Recovery/Stabilization (2016-2019), R3 COVID Shock (2020-2021), R4 Post-COVID/Russia-Ukraine (2022-2026). Portfolio: the 4-sleeve "all reference strategies" aggregate (Momentum, Carry F1-F3, CarryMom F1-F3, Value) combined via Equal Weight, Inverse Vol (63d), Risk Parity (ERC, rolling 252d/21d rebal), and Return-Tilt ERC (tilt=0.5, illustrative midpoint).

Expanding-window tables here use the same convention as the Metals report: cumulative-sample performance snapshots through each regime boundary (2011→2015, 2011→2019, 2011→2021, 2011→latest), NOT a train/test split — these are fixed ex-ante rule-based signals, nothing is refit between windows.

StatArb caveat: StatArb is NOT used for Precious Metals at all (STATARB_ENABLED = False, tested and rejected across all 10 pairs including Gold-Silver and Platinum-Palladium — a genuine null result from prior project work). This report covers exactly the 4 strategies the config defines; there is no StatArb sleeve to exclude-and-disclose the way there is for NGL/Energy.

Carry tenor liquidity caveat: the F1-F3 tenor was NOT re-verified for liquidity before this report was regenerated — Platinum and Palladium's Carry (F1-F3) and CarryMom (F1-F3) contributions rest on weaker real-volume support (87.0%/68.5% days traded) than Gold/Silver/Copper-COMEX's. This is a disclosed, not resolved, limitation of every number in this report that includes Carry or CarryMom.

6. Findings / synthesis

Moving Value's anchor toward the front of the curve still DECREASES Value's own standalone IR for Precious Metals — unchanged in direction from the F1-F2 version of this report. F8 +0.163 vs F1 +0.120, F2 +0.104, F3 +0.109. F8 (the baseline, further out on the curve) remains the strongest, and every front-of-curve variant is weaker. Value's own numbers did not change at all from the F1-F2 report (Carry doesn't feed into Value directly), so this finding is unchanged.

Carry and Carry-Momentum's own IR fell when the tenor moved from F1-F2 to F1-F3: Carry full-sample IR dropped from +0.134 (F1-F2) to +0.101 (F1-F3), and CarryMom flipped from positive to negative — +0.074 (F1-F2) to -0.058 (F1-F3). This is a real, material shift, not noise: both strategies got weaker, and CarryMom's sign flipped.

The single most consequential change from the F1-F2 version of this report: the one positive-crossing combine method is GONE. The old report's headline finding was that Precious Metals uniquely had Inverse Vol at +0.015 (net-positive) at the F8 baseline, with F1/F2/F3 all destroying that crossing. With Carry now at F1-F3, Inverse Vol at F8 has fallen to -0.066 — negative, like every other combine method at every other Value anchor. Every one of the 4 combine methods (Equal Weight, Inverse Vol, Risk Parity, Return-Tilt ERC) is now net-negative full-sample across all four Value variants (F8/F1/F2/F3) with no exception. Precious Metals' portfolio-level picture went from "one narrow escape hatch at baseline" to "uniformly negative everywhere" purely from the Carry tenor change — the Value-contract choice barely matters anymore at the portfolio level, since there is no longer any crossing for it to preserve or destroy.

Caveat: this is a first-order comparison against the F1-F2 version of this same report, not a claim about causation beyond "Carry got weaker and that dragged every combine method down with it" (plausible given Carry/CarryMom are 2 of the 4 sleeves in every combine method here). The F1-F3 tenor's own liquidity has not been independently re-verified (see the callout in Section 1.1) — if Platinum/Palladium's Carry (F1-F3) legs are genuinely as unreliable as the original real-volume check suggested F3 would be, some of this IR decline may reflect trading a thinly-traded tenor rather than a real economic finding.
