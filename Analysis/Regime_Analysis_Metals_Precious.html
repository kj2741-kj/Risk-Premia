Cross-product patterns (Metals + Precious Metals in context of all 20 products)

  • Vol regime — Energy is the outlier asset class: 5–6 of 7 Energy products have Momentum/Carry peaking in High vol, sharply different from Metals/Precious/NGL, which lean Low/Medium.
  • Curve-shape — Metals and NGL show the cleanest “Backwardation dominance” pattern (matching the original Copper pilot): both Metals products split Concave/Convex Backwardation only; NGL Carry V1 is Convex Backwardation for all 6 products, unanimous. Precious Metals and Energy are noisier/mixed between Contango and Backwardation.
  • Value's contango-preference (mirror image to trend/carry, as in the Copper pilot) holds loosely in Metals/NGL/Energy but not cleanly in Precious Metals.
  • 20 Bonferroni-significant cells total across all 20 products, heavily concentrated in NGL petrochemicals (Propylene: 6, Ethylene: 5) with large N (up to ~2,000 active days) — not small-sample flukes.
  • Caution: Palladium's one nominally-significant cell (Value × Medium vol, Sharpe 9.41) has only N=28, right at the minimum-N gate — treat as a small-sample artifact, not a confirmed finding.
  • No single cell/regime is significant across multiple different asset classes at once — every significant finding so far is asset-class-specific; nothing yet qualifies as a genuine cross-asset-class-confirmed universal signal.
