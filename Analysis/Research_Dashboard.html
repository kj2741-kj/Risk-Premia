Risk Premia Research Dashboard

Multi-Asset Systematic Risk Premia project. Ex-ante locked strategy backtests, log-return methodology.

Strategy definitions

Identical formulas and parameters across every asset class unless a tenor pair is noted otherwise.

Momentum: 3-way MA crossover composite

Three moving-average crossovers, MA(1,20), MA(5,60), MA(20,250) in trading days. Each produces a directional sign; the three signs are averaged and the sign of that average sets the position. Long when the composite trend is positive, short when negative.

Carry: V1 Level + V2 Z-score composite

Two views of the same curve slope (near tenor minus far tenor), averaged into one signal: V1 is the raw sign of the slope; V2 is that slope's z-score against its own 252-day rolling mean and standard deviation. Tenor pairs: F1-F3 and F1-F13 for Metals; F1-F2 for Precious Metals; F1-F3 and F1-F12 for Oil/Energy; F4-F15 for NGL (Bogorad's own convention).

Carry-Momentum: 20-day rate of change

Compares today's curve slope to its value 20 trading days earlier, capturing whether backwardation or contango is steepening or flattening. Reported as its own strategy, separate from the Carry composite above.

Value: F8, 1260-day lookback, 10% threshold

Compares the F8 contract's price to its own 1260-trading-day (five-year) moving average. A position is taken only when the deviation exceeds 10% in either direction.

EW Portfolio

Equal-weight combination, first across products within a strategy, then across all strategies. The benefit shown depends on how uncorrelated the underlying strategies are with one another.

StatArb: 20-day MA threshold on a spread

Same threshold-on-MA rule as Value, applied to a cross-asset-class spread instead of a single price: 20-day lookback, 10% deviation. Eight spreads, shared by Oil/Energy and NGL. Position PnL uses each leg's own log return, never the spread's own level, since a spread can be zero or negative.

StatArb sleeve (shared with NGL)

Eight cross-asset-class spreads, threshold-on-20-day-MA signal (10% deviation). Position PnL is each leg's own log return difference (position × (log-return of leg A − log-return of leg B)), not a log of the spread level itself, since a spread can be zero or negative while each leg's own price cannot. Spread levels (used only for the signal, not the PnL) are unit-converted where the two legs are quoted differently: RBOB and Heating Oil from cents/gallon to dollars, further to $/barrel (×42) against the crude benchmarks; Ethane from $/gallon to $/MMBtu (~66,000 BTU/gallon) against Nat Gas.

WTI's raw front-month price on 2020-04-20 (the famous brief negative settlement, -$37.63) is corrected at the source for the WTI-Brent and HeatingOil-WTI legs, imputed from Brent's own price that day minus the average WTI-Brent spread on the adjacent trading days. Momentum/Carry/Value are unaffected: their roll-adjusted continuous series had already switched to tracking the next contract five business days ahead of that expiry.

Benjamini-Hochberg FDR (q=0.10) across all 64 valid spread × window cells (8 spreads plus EW, full-sample and 7 regimes; NGL-legged spreads have no data in R1/R2): only the equal-weight combination's full-sample result survives (p=0.0005), plus one isolated regime cell (Propane-Butane in R3). No single spread clears the bar on its own -- the sleeve's significance comes from combining eight largely uncorrelated spreads, not from any one spread being individually strong.
