Risk Premia Research Dashboard

Multi-asset systematic risk premia research. Ex-ante locked strategy backtests, log-return methodology.

Each asset class is analyzed across four risk premia strategies -- Momentum, Carry, Carry-Momentum, and Value -- combined into two portfolio constructions: Equal Weight and Risk Parity. Where an asset class trades across two Carry tenor pairs, the tenor-level signals are equal-weighted into a single Carry and a single Carry-Momentum series before portfolio construction.

Methodology: Pearson correlations on daily net log-return series (Momentum, Carry, Carry-Momentum, Value, EW Portfolio, Risk Parity), sliced to the 2011-2026 reporting window. Within-asset-class correlations (Section 1) use each asset class's own trading calendar. Cross-commodity correlations (Section 2) are computed on the intersection of trading dates across all four asset classes (n=3,828 days), since Metals (LME), Oil/Energy (NYMEX/ICE), Precious Metals (COMEX/NYMEX), and NGL (Argus OTC) trade on different calendars.

Key patterns

Energy and NGL are not independent books. Their cross-commodity correlation is the highest off-diagonal pair for 5 of 6 strategy types -- Value (0.53), Momentum (0.45), Risk Parity (0.31), EW PORT (0.28), Carry (0.23) -- confirming both trade on shared hydrocarbon-complex drivers. Sizing both asset classes up together should account for this shared exposure.

Metals and Precious Metals show a smaller but consistent correlation across every strategy type (~0.30-0.36 for Momentum, Carry, Value, EW PORT, and Risk Parity) -- a real, if modest, shared-factor exposure despite very different fundamental drivers.

Carry-Momentum is the most diversifying strategy across commodities -- its cross-commodity correlations are the lowest of any strategy (mostly under 0.16 in magnitude), unlike Value and Momentum which both carry meaningful cross-commodity correlation.

Value is the primary diversifier within each asset class, showing low or negative correlation to the Equal Weight portfolio (Metals 0.04, Energy -0.19, NGL -0.02; Precious Metals is the exception at 0.37). Risk Parity is consistently more correlated with the Equal Weight portfolio than any individual strategy (0.78-0.96 across all four asset classes), as expected given both are built from the same four underlying strategies.

Built Cross-Commodity Portfolio: all 4 strategies, across all 4 asset classes

Two-stage construction. First, each of the four base strategies (Momentum, Carry, Carry-Momentum, Value) is equal-weighted across the four asset classes, producing one cross-commodity series per strategy. Second, those four cross-commodity strategy series are combined into an Equal Weight portfolio and a Risk Parity portfolio (rolling Equal Risk Contribution, 252-day covariance window, ~21-day rebalance). This hierarchical approach -- equal-weighting within a strategy across commodities, then combining across strategies -- differs from a flat optimization over all sixteen underlying asset-class/strategy legs simultaneously.

Computed on the intersection of trading dates across all four asset classes (n=3,828 days), since Metals (LME), Oil/Energy (NYMEX/ICE), Precious Metals (COMEX/NYMEX), and NGL (Argus OTC) trade on different calendars.

Strategy definitions

Identical formulas and parameters across every asset class unless a tenor pair is noted otherwise. Where an asset class has two Carry tenor pairs (Metals, Oil/Energy: F1-F3 & F1-F13; NGL: F2-F4 & F2-F14), the Carry and Carry-Momentum rows shown are each an equal-weight combination of the two tenor-level signals.

Momentum: 3-way MA crossover composite

Three moving-average crossovers, MA(1,20), MA(5,60), MA(20,250) in trading days. Each produces a directional sign; the three signs are averaged and the sign of that average sets the position. Long when the composite trend is positive, short when negative.

Carry: V1 Level + V2 Z-score, EW across tenors

Within one tenor pair: two views of that tenor's curve slope (near minus far), averaged into one signal -- V1 is the raw sign of the slope, V2 is that slope's z-score against its own 252-day rolling mean/std. Where an asset class has two tenor pairs, that per-tenor composite is computed separately for each pair, then the two tenor-level composites are equal-weighted into the single Carry row shown. Precious Metals has only one tenor pair (F1-F3), so no cross-tenor combination applies there.

Carry-Momentum: 20-day rate of change, EW across tenors

Compares today's curve slope to its value 20 trading days earlier, capturing whether backwardation or contango is steepening or flattening -- computed per tenor pair, then equal-weighted across tenor pairs the same way as Carry above. Reported as its own strategy, separate from the Carry composite.

Value: F3, 1260-day lookback, 10% threshold

Compares the F3 contract's price to its own 1260-trading-day (five-year) moving average. A position is taken only when the deviation exceeds 10% in either direction.

EW Portfolio

Equal-weight combination of the four strategies above (Momentum, Carry, Carry-Momentum, Value), applied after equal-weighting across products within each strategy.

Risk Parity (ERC, rolling)

Equal Risk Contribution portfolio across the same four strategies: weights are solved from a trailing 252-day covariance matrix so each strategy contributes equal risk, rebalanced every 21 trading days with no look-ahead. Falls back to equal weight before a full 252-day window is available.
