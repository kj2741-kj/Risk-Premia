Risk Premia Research Dashboard (Combined Carry + Risk Parity)

Multi-Asset Systematic Risk Premia project. Ex-ante locked strategy backtests, log-return methodology.

How this differs from Research_Dashboard_F1F13.html (for comparison, not a replacement): that dashboard shows each Carry/CarryMom tenor pair as its own row (e.g. "Carry (F1-F3)" and "Carry (F1-F13)" separately). This dashboard instead equal-weights those tenor-level composites into exactly ONE Carry row and ONE CarryMom row per asset class -- giving exactly 4 main risk premia per tab (Momentum, Carry, CarryMom, Value) plus 2 portfolio-level combinations (EW and Risk Parity). Precious Metals only ever had one tenor pair (F1-F3), so its numbers are identical in both dashboards. Same underlying strategy legs, same tenor choices, same product filters (5-product Energy, 4-product NGL) as Research_Dashboard_F1F13.html -- only the Carry/CarryMom presentation (combined vs. separate) differs.

Methodology: Pearson correlations on daily NET log-return series (the same 6 series -- Momentum, Carry, CarryMom, Value, EW PORT, Risk Parity -- underlying every tab above), sliced to the standardized reporting window (2011-01-01 onward). Within-asset-class (Section 1) uses each asset class's own trading calendar directly. Cross-commodity (Section 2) aligns all four asset classes on the INTERSECTION of trading dates only (n=3,828 days) -- Metals (LME), Oil/Energy (NYMEX/ICE), Precious Metals (COMEX/NYMEX), and NGL (Argus OTC) trade on different calendars, so an inner join is the only fair comparison, rather than silently treating "exchange closed" as "flat that day."

Section 1: Within-Asset-Class Correlation

How correlated are the 4 base strategies and 2 portfolio combinations with EACH OTHER, inside one asset class? High EW/Risk Parity correlation with an individual strategy indicates concentration risk (the "portfolio" isn't diversifying much); low or negative Value correlation with the other three is the diversification benefit Value is supposed to provide.

Key patterns

Energy and NGL are not independent books. Their cross-commodity correlation is the highest off-diagonal pair for 5 of 6 strategy types -- Value (0.53), Momentum (0.45), Risk Parity (0.31), EW PORT (0.28), Carry (0.23) -- confirming both trade on shared hydrocarbon-complex drivers. Sizing both asset classes up together should account for this shared exposure.

Metals and Precious Metals show a smaller but consistent correlation across every strategy type (~0.30-0.36 for Momentum, Carry, Value, EW PORT, and Risk Parity) -- a real, if modest, shared-factor exposure despite very different fundamental drivers.

CarryMom is the most genuinely diversifying strategy across commodities -- its cross-commodity correlations are the lowest of any strategy (mostly under 0.16 in magnitude), unlike Value and Momentum which both carry meaningful cross-commodity correlation.

Within every asset class, Value is the true diversifier against EW PORT (Metals 0.04, Energy -0.19, NGL -0.02; only Precious Metals breaks the pattern at 0.37). Risk Parity is consistently MORE correlated with EW PORT than any individual strategy (0.78-0.96 across all four) -- expected, since both are built from the same 4 legs, just weighted differently.

Built Cross-Commodity Portfolio: all 4 strategies, across all 4 asset classes

Two-stage construction. Stage 1: each of the 4 base strategies (Momentum, Carry, CarryMom, Value -- same series as every asset-class tab above) is equal-weighted ACROSS the 4 asset classes (Metals, Oil/Energy, Precious Metals, NGL), giving one cross-commodity Momentum series, one cross-commodity Carry series, etc. Stage 2: those 4 cross-commodity strategy series are combined into EW PORT (equal weight) and Risk Parity (rolling ERC, same 252-day/~21-day/tilt=0 construction as every other Risk Parity row in this dashboard). This is a hierarchical combination -- first EW across commodities within a strategy, then EW or Risk Parity across strategies -- not a single flat optimization over all 16 underlying asset-class x strategy legs at once.

Cross-commodity combination is computed on the INTERSECTION of trading dates across all 4 asset classes (n=3,828 days, same convention as the Correlations tab), since Metals (LME), Oil/Energy (NYMEX/ICE), Precious Metals (COMEX/NYMEX), and NGL (Argus OTC) trade on different calendars.

Strategy definitions

Identical formulas and parameters across every asset class unless a tenor pair is noted otherwise. Where an asset class has two Carry tenor pairs (Metals, Oil/Energy: F1-F3 & F1-F13; NGL: F2-F4 & F2-F14), the Carry and CarryMom rows shown are each an equal-weight combination of the two tenor-level signals -- see each tab's own note for the exact pairs.

Momentum: 3-way MA crossover composite

Three moving-average crossovers, MA(1,20), MA(5,60), MA(20,250) in trading days. Each produces a directional sign; the three signs are averaged and the sign of that average sets the position. Long when the composite trend is positive, short when negative.

Carry: V1 Level + V2 Z-score, EW across tenors

Within one tenor pair: two views of that tenor's curve slope (near minus far), averaged into one signal -- V1 is the raw sign of the slope, V2 is that slope's z-score against its own 252-day rolling mean/std. Where an asset class has two tenor pairs, that per-tenor composite is computed separately for each pair, then the two tenor-level composites are equal-weighted into the single Carry row shown. Precious Metals has only one tenor pair (F1-F3), so no cross-tenor combination applies there.

Carry-Momentum: 20-day rate of change, EW across tenors

Compares today's curve slope to its value 20 trading days earlier, capturing whether backwardation or contango is steepening or flattening -- computed per tenor pair, then equal-weighted across tenor pairs the same way as Carry above. Reported as its own strategy, separate from the Carry composite.

Value: F3, 1260-day lookback, 10% threshold

Compares the F3 contract's price to its own 1260-trading-day (five-year) moving average. A position is taken only when the deviation exceeds 10% in either direction.

EW Portfolio

Equal-weight combination of exactly the 4 rows above (Momentum, Carry, CarryMom, Value) -- first across products within each strategy (as usual), then equal-weighted across these 4 strategies. Unlike Research_Dashboard_F1F13.html's EW PORT, which averages across every separately-disclosed tenor row (5-6 legs depending on asset class), this EW PORT always averages exactly 4 legs, matching the "4 main risk premia" framing of this dashboard.

Risk Parity (ERC, rolling)

Equal Risk Contribution portfolio across the SAME 4 rows (Momentum, Carry, CarryMom, Value): weights solved from the trailing 252-day covariance matrix so each of the 4 strategies contributes equal risk, rebalanced every ~21 trading days, no look-ahead. Falls back to equal weight before the first full 252-day window is available. Same defaults (window=252, rebalance=21, tilt=0 i.e. pure ERC) as the Streamlit Portfolio tabs' "Risk Parity (ERC, rolling)" option -- see research/risk_parity.py.
