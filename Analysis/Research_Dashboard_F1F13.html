Ethylene and Propylene are excluded from this dashboard (2026-08-03, per decision); both remain in the underlying research config/engine and are unaffected there. Removing them actually widened the common date range (2009-11-10 vs. the prior 6-product window's 2010-12-13 start) -- Ethylene and Propylene were the two products whose own later history was pulling the common start date forward.

Changed in this variant: Carry tenors are F2-F4 (short) and F2-F14 (long), not the single F4-F15 tenor (Bogorad's own convention, front-month prices being stale/index-settled for this asset class) -- same two-tenor pattern as Metals (F1-F3/F1-F13) and Oil/Energy (F1-F3/F1-F13), reported as separate rows below rather than blended together.

Each Carry (F2-Fn) row is itself an equal-weight (EW) portfolio of two views of that tenor's curve slope: V1 (Level, the raw sign of the slope) and V2 (Z-score, that slope's own 252-day rolling z-score) -- identical construction to every other Carry row in this dashboard (see the Strategy definitions card below), just applied separately to F2-F4 and to F2-F14 instead of to F4-F15. CarryMom (F2-Fn) is the 20-day rate-of-change signal on that same tenor's slope, also reported once per tenor pair, not V1/V2-composited (Carry-Momentum was never a V1+V2 blend in this dashboard's methodology, for any asset class). Both tenors are well-populated for all four products here (F2/F4/F14 all ≥99.5% quoted) -- no Fuel-Oil-F12 or Platinum/Palladium-F6+ style data-quality gap on either leg. This is a standalone sensitivity variant; the locked research config (research/configs/ngl.py) is untouched and still specifies the single F4-F15 tenor.

Strategy definitions

Identical formulas and parameters across every asset class unless a tenor pair is noted otherwise.

Momentum: 3-way MA crossover composite

Three moving-average crossovers, MA(1,20), MA(5,60), MA(20,250) in trading days. Each produces a directional sign; the three signs are averaged and the sign of that average sets the position. Long when the composite trend is positive, short when negative.

Carry: V1 Level + V2 Z-score composite

Two views of the same curve slope (near tenor minus far tenor), each tenor pair's Carry row is an equal-weight (EW) portfolio of these two views: V1 is the raw sign of the slope; V2 is that slope's z-score against its own 252-day rolling mean and standard deviation. Tenor pairs: F1-F3 and F1-F13 for Metals; F1-F3 for Precious Metals (changed from F1-F2 in this variant); F1-F3 and F1-F13 for Oil/Energy (changed from F1-F12 in this variant, 5-product set only); F2-F4 and F2-F14 for NGL (changed from the single F4-F15 tenor in this variant, 4-product set) -- reported as two separate rows per asset class, same pattern throughout, not blended across tenors.

Carry-Momentum: 20-day rate of change

Compares today's curve slope to its value 20 trading days earlier, capturing whether backwardation or contango is steepening or flattening. Reported as its own strategy, separate from the Carry composite above.

Value: F3, 1260-day lookback, 10% threshold

Compares the F3 contract's price to its own 1260-trading-day (five-year) moving average. A position is taken only when the deviation exceeds 10% in either direction. (Changed from F8 to F3 2026-08-03, applied uniformly across all four asset classes -- unchanged from the baseline dashboard.)

EW Portfolio

Equal-weight combination, first across products within a strategy, then across all strategies. The benefit shown depends on how uncorrelated the underlying strategies are with one another.
