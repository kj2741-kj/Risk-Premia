Strategy definitions

Identical formulas and parameters across every asset class unless a tenor pair is noted otherwise.

Momentum: 3-way MA crossover composite

Three moving-average crossovers, MA(1,20), MA(5,60), MA(20,250) in trading days. Each produces a directional sign; the three signs are averaged and the sign of that average sets the position. Long when the composite trend is positive, short when negative.

Carry: V1 Level + V2 Z-score composite

Two views of the same curve slope (near tenor minus far tenor), averaged into one signal: V1 is the raw sign of the slope; V2 is that slope's z-score against its own 252-day rolling mean and standard deviation. Tenor pairs: F1-F3 and F1-F13 for Metals; F1-F2 for Precious Metals; F1-F3 and F1-F12 for Oil/Energy; F4-F15 for NGL (Bogorad's own convention).

Carry-Momentum: 20-day rate of change

Compares today's curve slope to its value 20 trading days earlier, capturing whether backwardation or contango is steepening or flattening. Reported as its own strategy, separate from the Carry composite above.

Value: F3, 1260-day lookback, 10% threshold

Compares the F3 contract's price to its own 1260-trading-day (five-year) moving average. A position is taken only when the deviation exceeds 10% in either direction. (Changed from F8 to F3 2026-08-03, applied uniformly across all four asset classes.)

EW Portfolio

Equal-weight combination, first across products within a strategy, then across all strategies. The benefit shown depends on how uncorrelated the underlying strategies are with one another.
