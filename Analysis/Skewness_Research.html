Skewness style: research page

Separate from the Final dashboard. Generated 2026-10-12.

Method

Per product, each day: signal = minus the skewness of the continuous series' daily log returns over the past 252 observations. Position +1 when skewness is negative, -1 when positive (Fernandez-Perez, Fuertes and Miffre 2021, Table 1: the Skewness style is long negatively skewed contracts). Same engine as the other sleeves: two-day execution lag, 5 bps round-trip costs plus the roll-day charge, roll-adjusted continuous series, 5th-trading-day-of-expiry-month roll. Products equal-weighted within the asset class; portfolios by Equal Weight or Inverse Vol (trailing 63 days, lagged one day). Basis: the 2012 variant (Value needs 504 observations), reported from 1 Jan 2012 to 23 Sep 2026 in three periods. The paper's own version is cross-sectional (long/short sort across contracts, held a month); this is the time-series version used by all our sleeves, so numbers are not comparable with the paper's.
