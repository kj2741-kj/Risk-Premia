Volatility-timing integration (VTI): research page

Separate from the Final dashboard. Generated 2026-10-12.

Method

VTI (Kirby and Ostdiek 2012; Fernandez-Perez, Fuertes and Miffre 2021, eq. 3) weights each style by the inverse of its return variance, normalised to sum to 1, with variance measured over the last 60 monthly observations and weights reset at month-end for the next month. The existing Inverse Vol uses the inverse of volatility over 63 days, updated daily, so VTI concentrates more on the least volatile style. Window adaptation: expanding until 60 months, at least 36 months; a style's months count from its first non-zero return; in any month where a style has fewer than 36 months, or zero variance, all styles get equal weights (share of such months in the last column below). Monthly returns are the sum of the daily net log returns. Basis: the 2012 variant, reported from 1 Jan 2012 to 23 Sep 2026 in three periods; the sleeves are the Final dashboard's, with Skewness as a possible fifth.
